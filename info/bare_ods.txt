## 13. SHOOTING PHILOSOPHY / OPERATIONAL NOTES

- Preferred shooting times: sunrise and sunset (midday avoided due to unflattering light)
- In bad weather: team waits for weather to clear, or offers to continue/relocate
- Couple sessions do not include midday shooting as standard practice
