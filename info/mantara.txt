## TERMS & CONDITIONS

- **Booking Fee (Down Payment):** 50% of package price
- Booking fee is non-refundable upon date cancellation; rescheduling is allowed minimum 2 weeks before the shoot date
- **Final Payment:** 1 week (7 days) before the shoot date
- A deposit/down payment is required as confirmation of the event date
- Payment process is completed after file delivery is confirmed
- Editing is processed on a queue system; maximum delivery time is 1 month from D+1 of the event, or as per prior agreement
- Maximum 2 revisions included; additional revisions are subject to extra fees
- Working hours are as stated in each package; additional time is charged per hour
