## TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. Booking and Payment
   - Down payment (DP) of 30% of total package price is required upon booking confirmation
   - Full payment must be settled no later than 7 days before the shoot session
   - DP is non-refundable in the event of cancellation by the client

2. Location and Scheduling
   - Shoot location is agreed upon between client and vendor
   - Schedule changes by the client are allowed up to 1 time, with at least 30 days' prior notice
   - If the client cancels unilaterally with less than 30 days' notice, payments already made are non-refundable

3. Duration and Photo Count
   - Session duration and number of photos are as specified in the chosen package
   - Client will receive edited photos as specified in the selected package

4. Copyright and Photo Usage
   - Vendor retains the right to use photos for portfolio, promotion, and social media, unless otherwise agreed in writing
   - Client may use photos for personal purposes without removing any watermark (if applicable)

5. Additional Fees
   - Additional charges apply for locations outside the designated service area
   - Additional services such as MUA, special props, or extra outfits will incur additional fees as agreed

6. Force Majeure
   - In the event of natural disasters, pandemics, or other conditions preventing the shoot, the schedule may be rescheduled at no additional cost

7. General
   - Terms and conditions may change at any time without prior notice
   - By placing a booking, the client is deemed to have read and agreed to all applicable terms
